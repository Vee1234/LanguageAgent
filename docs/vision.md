# Vision & Mission — Own Flashcard App

## Vision

Knowing a word means knowing more than its translation. It means knowing how formal it
is, what it implies, where it has been used, and when a native speaker would choose it.
Language learning should build that depth, then carry it into real reading and
conversation. It should make learning a language fun, accessible and immersive, and save language
learners time.

## Mission

Build a personal, phone-first language app that:

- **Captures** words wherever I meet them: typed in, picked out of a screenshot, or
  suggested from the words I already know.
- **Enriches** each word automatically with its definition, examples, register,
  connotations, notable uses and song lyrics, alongside my own notes.
- **Schedules** it for spaced-repetition review, so it actually sticks.
- **Puts it to use** through register ranking, comprehension questions on real texts,
  and spoken conversations with an AI that marks my grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation.

It should be free to run, private by default, and easy for others to fork and host
themselves.

# Features to add:

**Guiding principle:** the app should feel like taking the user through a story, on a
journey, not like working through a stack of disconnected cards.

1. You can attach your own notes / generated notes to every word you add. Register, famous uses, examples, description come already added.
2. For register practice, you could rank a word and its generated synonyms in terms of register.
3. You provide a screenshot of a page with highlitighted/underlined words, it extracts the words and adds the words to words you want to learn.
4. Words to learn can be grouped into decks, but each automatically has a flashcard created for it.
5. Based on the words you already know, the app finds the words you don't know yet and adds them to a deck. 
6. Have audio chats with the ai and get marked at the end- grammar / pronunciation mistakes analysed, strengths identified and excellent vocabulary highlighted. Could even suggest more natural ways of saying things based on the situation.
7. Song lyrics retrieved from the musixmatch api. 
8. Provides comprehension questions based on a text that you provide (scope in the future for it source these articles themselves). The comprehension questions will check that the user has got the gist of the article, but also has not misunderstood any key details, understands why the article is culturally important and understands key bits of vocab (eg. which word in the article denotes xyz, is a synonym of abc, what is the meaning of def). 
9. Generate decks from a user prompt (e.g. "words for ordering at a restaurant"), drawing on the user's existing words plus new ones.
10. Browse and search public decks that other users have made, and add them to your own collection.
11. Clip suggestions: short video/audio clips where a word is used in context. Clips are linked, not hosted: the app stores the URL (and start time) and shows the clip in an embedded player, e.g. a YouTube iframe.
